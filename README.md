# DealFlow360

An Intelligent, Self-Governing B2B Sales Operations Platform.

## System Overview
DealFlow360 is an intelligent, self-governing B2B Sales Operations Platform covering the complete business lifecycle:
- **Authentication & Role-Based Access Control** (Neeraj)
- **Product & Commercial Pricing Configuration** (Neeraj)
- **Quotation Creation & Management** (Atharva)
- **Discount Governance & Blended Risk Analysis** (Neeraj)
- **Automated Approval Routing** (Neeraj)
- **Customer Negotiation Portal** (Neeraj)
- **Upsell & Cross-Sell Intelligence** (Atharva)
- **Live Margin Impact Monitoring** (Atharva)
- **Multi-Warehouse Fulfillment & Order Splitting** (Vignesh)
- **Hybrid Billing & Subscriptions** (Vignesh)
- **Payment Tracking** (Vignesh)
- **Deal Health Monitoring & Anomaly Signals** (Atharva)
- **Audit Trails & Reporting** (Vignesh / Neeraj)

## Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: React, TypeScript, Vite, Vanilla CSS
- **Backend**: Node.js, TypeScript, Express.js
- **Database**: PostgreSQL (Containerized via Docker)
